              Linked calendars

              See events and deadlines from Google Calendar, Outlook, Apple iCloud, Canvas or any calendar that has a sharing link. They show up in Tasks.

                Events leave Tasks once they're over. To-dos, like assignments, stay until you tick them off.

                Where do I find the link?
                • Google Calendar (on a computer): Settings → click your calendar under "Settings for my calendars" → copy Secret address in iCal format.
                • Outlook: Settings → Calendar → Shared calendars → Publish a calendar → pick the calendar and "Can view all details" → Publish → copy the ICS link.
                • Apple iCloud: in the Calendar app, share the calendar, turn on Public Calendar and copy the link.
                • Canvas: Calendar → Calendar Feed (bottom right) → copy the link.

                Keep these links private. Anyone who has a link can see that calendar.
